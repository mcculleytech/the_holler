# overview

`the holler` is an appalachian take on the story of peer gynt by henrik ibsen and the music by edvard grieg. Inspired by my son's love of the music and having read some short stories by breece pancake (the inspiration for appalachia). Notably, this will be a simplification of the Mountain King saga of the peer gynt story. 

## layout
The story will follow peter greg and will consist of seven levels:

1. The Wedding 
2. The Lake
3. The Entrance to the Mountain
4. The Upper Levels of the Mountain
5. The Heart of the Mountain
6. The Escape
7. The Return

### the wedding

Peter Greg is at a wedding with his mother. His mother asks him to help setup for the reception in the holler. He must clear the glade of animals, setup chairs in a specific arrangement, and another task to be determined.
